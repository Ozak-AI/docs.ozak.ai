Welcome to Ozak AI


Ozak AI is an agentic AI platform that leverages predictive AI purpose-built for financial markets.


By fusing state-of-the-art machine-learning models with decentralized infrastructure, Ozak AI delivers real-time, tamper-proof intelligence that helps individuals and institutions anticipate market shifts, mitigate risk, and unlock new trading strategies.


Built for investors and institutions, it delivers real-time, AI-driven insights using models like neural networks, ARIMA, and linear regression to generate accurate market forecasts and risk assessments. Its decentralized backbone includes EigenLayer AVS for secure, trustless validation and Arbitrum Orbit for efficient smart contract execution. 


## Why Ozak AI Exists


Legacy trading dashboards were built for slow batch jobs - not for the millisecond-level swings of modern crypto and global markets. They stop at the exchange API and can’t merge order-book noise with on-chain liquidity, macro feeds, or sentiment data into a single stream of actionable financial intelligence. Ozak AI closes that gap.


### Accuracy at speed


Neural networks, ARIMA, and custom time-series models ingest billions of ticks, funding-rate flips, and alt-data points in real time.


### Unified, verifiable intelligence


EigenLayer AVS attaches economic collateral to every prediction, turning raw model output into a trustless data feed that both humans and smart contracts can rely on - no opaque middle layer.


### Scalability without gas pain


Arbitrum Orbit shifts heavy compute off L1 while anchoring results to Ethereum, so simulating hundreds of assets costs pennies, not dollars.
