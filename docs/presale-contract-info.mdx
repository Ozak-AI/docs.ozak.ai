6. Purchase Flow Recap


1. Call `buyTokensETH()` (or approve + `buyTokens()`).


2. Contract emits `TokensPurchased` (includes amount, wallet, phase).


3. After TGE, users call `claim` → OZ appears in wallet.

8. Gas-Saving Tips


Batch buys: Buying once per phase costs less gas than many small buys.


Use ETH where possible: ERC-20 approvals add an extra transaction.


9. FAQ (Contract Edition)


Q: Can I call the contract directly from Etherscan?


A: Yes - paste the verified address into Etherscan, choose “Write Contract,” connect your


wallet, and invoke `buyTokensETH()` or `buyTokens()`.


Q: What happens if the presale hard cap isn’t reached?


A: Unsold tokens are automatically sent to the Ecosystem pool after the claim window closes.
