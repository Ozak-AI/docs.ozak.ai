Relax - your tokens are safe and already sitting in your personal dashboard.

1\. **Open your dashboard:**
Head to [app.ozak.ai](https://app.ozak.ai) dashboard using the same wallet you joined the presale with. Your current $OZ balance is shown there in real time.

2\. **When can I claim them?**
As soon as the presale closes and we hit **TGE (Token Generation Event)**, a “Claim” button will appear beside your balance. Click it and choose any compatible wallet; the tokens move on-chain in one transaction.

3\. **Double-check your purchase:**
\- Make sure your presale transaction shows “Success” in your wallet or on the relevant block explorer.
\- If you used a different wallet address than the one connected to the dashboard, you’ll need to reconnect with that same address to see your      tokens.
