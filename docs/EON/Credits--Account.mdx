title: Credits & Account
---

Eon runs on a credit-based system. Running advanced AI models, scanning blockchains in real-time, and generating deep-dive reports requires significant computing power. Credits are the fuel that powers these interactions.

**Understanding Your Balance**

When you create a new account, you are given a **Welcome Balance of 10,000 Credits** for 7 days.

**Bonus:** You can earn an additional **2,000 Credits** immediately by completing the short user survey upon your first login.

How Credits are used:

Every interaction with Eon consumes a small amount of credits. Simple text chats cost less, while complex tasks- like generating a full Fundamental Analysis report or running a multi-indicator Technical scan - cost more.

**How to Top Up**

If you run out of fuel, you don't have to stop working. We have added a **Top Up** feature directly into Eon..

**Go to Profile:** Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar.

**Select "Top up Credits":** This will open the payment window.

**Choose Your Pack:** Select your preferred chain, currency, and the amount.

**Proceed to Pay:** We currently support payments via:

**Ethereum Mainnet:** ETH, USDC, USDT

**BNB Chain:** BNB, USDC, USDT

*Note: Make sure you copy the exact wallet address displayed on your screen and pay from the chain you selected before.*
