Getting into Eon is designed to be as frictionless as possible while keeping your account secure. You have multiple ways to access your dashboard.

#### **How to Login**

We offer three primary methods to sign in. Choose the one that fits your workflow best:

**Social Login:** The fastest way in. You can use your existing **Google** account. Just click the “Continue with Google” icon, authorize the app, and you’re in. This is perfect if you want to get started immediately without managing another password.

**Web3 Wallet:** For the crypto-native users. You can connect your wallet (such as **MetaMask**, **Rabby**, or **WalletConnect**). This is the recommended method if you plan to use features that require on-chain verification later.

**Email (OTP):** Classic and secure. Enter your email address, and we will send you a one-time password (OTP). No need to remember complex passwords; just check your inbox and verify.

**Safety First: Understanding Wallet Permissions**

If you choose to connect via a Web3 Wallet, you might see a pop-up asking for permission. We know this can be scary, so let’s clarify what we ask for:

**Sign to Verify (Safe):** When you first login, we ask you to "Sign" a message. **This costs $0 gas.** It is simply a cryptographic handshake to prove that you own the wallet address. **It does *not* give us access to your funds.**

**Transaction Request (Requires Attention):** You will only see a transaction request if you are actively buying something (like topping up Credits). This will always show you the exact amount (e.g., $50 USDC) and the gas fee.

**We will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.** If anyone claiming to be from Ozak AI asks for this, it is a scam.
