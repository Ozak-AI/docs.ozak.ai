---
title: The Explore Menu (Analysis Tools)
---

Clicking the **Explore** button on the left sidebar opens your main toolkit. This is where you go when you need to answer the question "Why?" in detail.

The Explore menu is broken down into specific sections, each designed to handle a different part of your research.

**Market Overview**

This is your high-level map.

**The Chart:** Shows you the global market cap, volume, Bitcoin dominance trends, and so much more.

**The AI Narrative:** This is the magic part. Below every chart, you will see an **"AI Insights"** box. Instead of just showing you a line going down, Eon reads the market data and explains *why* it’s happening (e.g., "Whale accumulation," "Sector rotation," or "Retail panic"). It turns raw data into a story.

**Fundamental & Technical Analysis**

We automated the "grunt work" of research. You no longer need to visit 10 different websites to check a coin’s health.

**Fundamental Analysis:** Focuses on the "true value."

**How to use:** Select a token (e.g., BTC) → Select a metric (e.g., *Circulating Supply vs Total Supply*, *Top Holder Concentration*, or *Dev Activity*) → Click **Get Analysis**.

**The Result:** You get a clear written summary of the project's health.

**Technical Analysis:** Focuses on price action.

**How to use:** Select a token → Select an indicator (e.g., *RSI*, *Moving Averages*, or *Auto Support/Resistance*) → Click **Get Analysis**.

**The Result:** Eon tells you if the asset is overbought, oversold, or hitting a critical price wall. No need to draw lines on a chart yourself.

**Calendars**

Timing is everything. We split this into two views:

**Event Calendar:** Tracks crypto-specific events like **Mainnet Launches**, **Governance Votes**, and **AMAs**. These are potential catalysts for a price pump.

**Economic Calendar:** Tracks global macro events like **CPI (Inflation) Data**, **Interest Rate Decisions**, and **GDP Reports**. These affect the entire market (especially Bitcoin).

**Token Unlocks**

This tool is your shield against sudden price drops. It tracks when projects release locked tokens into the market (which often causes the price to dump).

**Monthly View:** The bar chart at the top shows you the "Danger Days" - dates with massive unlock volumes.

**Daily Breakdown:** Click on any specific day to see exactly which coins are unlocking and how much value (in millions of dollars) is hitting the market.

**Why use it?** Check this calendar *before* you buy. You don't want to buy a coin today if 10% of its supply unlocks tomorrow.

**News Feed & Sentiment**

Stop doom-scrolling.

**Sentiment Analysis:** We visualize the "mood" of the market using bar charts. You can see at a glance if the news for a specific coin is **Positive** (Bullish) or **Negative** (Bearish) over the last 7 days.

**Smart Feed:** Filter news by "Bullish" or "Bearish" to cut through the noise and see only what matters to your trade.
