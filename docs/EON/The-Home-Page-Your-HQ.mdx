The Home Page is where it all comes together. It is designed to be clean and distraction-free, putting the most powerful tools right in front of you without the clutter.

**The Eon Input Bar**

At the center of the screen is the **Eon Input Bar**. This is where you talk to the Eon.

You don't need to learn special commands or code. Just type like you are texting a friend. Eon understands context, slang, and specific crypto terminology.

**Try asking:** *"Is Bitcoin looking bullish today?"*

**Try asking:** *"Summarize the latest news for Ethereum."*

**Try asking:** *"What are the key support levels for SOL?"*

**Choosing Your AI Agent**

Not every question needs the same kind of answer. A trader looks at a coin differently than a long-term investor. That is why Eon lets you switch Personas.

Located just below the input bar, this dropdown menu lets you choose *who* answers your question:

**@Global Crypto:** The generalist. Perfect for broad market questions, news summaries, and "big picture" trends. Use this for your daily catch-up.

**@Technical Analyst:** The chart watcher. This AI Agent focuses purely on price action, indicators (RSI, MACD), and market structure. It gives you support/resistance levels and trend analysis.

**@Fundamental Analyst:** The researcher. This AI Agent ignores the hourly price swings and looks at the health of the project. It analyzes tokenomics, developer activity, on-chain volume, and whitepaper details.

**Market Vitals**

Below the input bar, you will find a quick snapshot of the market's pulse. We keep this simple so you can gauge the "vibe" in seconds.

**Fear & Greed Index:** A simple gauge telling you if the market is panicking (Fear) or getting too excited (Greed).

**Quick Updates:** A curated feed of the most critical market movements happening right now, so you don't miss major events while you are typing.
