---
title: Threads (Managing Intelligence)
---

You can’t keep everything in your head. As you use Eon, you will generate a lot of insights - chart setups, token deep dives, and market summaries. We built tools to make sure you never lose that work.

Every time you ask Eon a question, that conversation is saved automatically as a **Thread**.

**How it works:** Think of Threads like a history of your thought process. If you spend 20 minutes analyzing Ethereum's layer-2 fees today, you don't want to start from scratch tomorrow.

**Accessing History:** Click the **Threads** icon on the left sidebar. This opens a list of your past conversations.

**Pick Up Where You Left Off:** Clicking a thread instantly restores the entire chat context. The AI remembers what you were talking about, so you can continue the conversation seamlessly.
