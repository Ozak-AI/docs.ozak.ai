### How it works

| | |
|---|---|
| How you earn | Complete quests, each worth a set amount of XP |
| How it pays out | Your share of the total XP earned by everyone |
| When | Calculated and distributed at the Token Generation Event |

**Worked example.** If the whole community earns 100,000 XP and you earned 1,000 of it, that is 1% of the total, so you receive 1% of the pool - $10,000 worth of $OZ.

:::info Your share is relative
Because payouts are proportional, your reward depends on both your own XP and everyone else's. Earning early and consistently matters more than a single big push.
:::

## Ranks

Your rank is based on the **bought USD value of the tokens in your presale wallet**. Direct purchases only.

| Rank | Total purchased | What it means and unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Omega | $0 to $99 | The Curious Observer. A starting point for exploring the ecosystem |
| Delta | $100 to $999 | The Diligent Learner. Puts you on the public leaderboard, with early access to community AMAs planned |
| Beta | $1,000 to $9,999 | The Strategic Player. Unlocks priority support and first access to beta-test new features |
| Alpha | $10,000 to $49,999 | The Influential Leader. Earns a VIP role in community channels and a larger share of future giveaways |
| Sigma | $50,000+ | The Visionary. Exclusive access to the team and priority access to future airdrops |

:::info Purchases add up
Your rank uses your cumulative total, not individual transactions. Two separate $600 purchases move you from Delta to Beta.
:::

Your current standing is on the live leaderboard at [presale.ozak.ai](https://presale.ozak.ai).
