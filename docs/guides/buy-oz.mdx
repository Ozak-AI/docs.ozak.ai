## Step 1 - get your wallet ready

Your wallet is where your $OZ will eventually live.

:::danger Never share your seed phrase
Your Secret Recovery Phrase is the master key to your funds. Write it down, store it offline, and never type it into a website or send it to anyone. Nobody from Ozak AI will ever ask for it.
:::

If you do not have a wallet yet, MetaMask is the straightforward choice:

1. Download it from the official site, [metamask.io](https://metamask.io)
2. Follow the setup and create a new wallet
3. Write down your Secret Recovery Phrase and store it offline
4. Confirm the network is set to **Ethereum Mainnet**

## Step 2 - fund your wallet

You can pay with **ETH, USDT or USDC**, plus a little extra ETH for gas.

**Buying with a card**

The simplest route if you are new. Use a reputable on-ramp to buy crypto with a debit or credit card and send it to your wallet address, the one beginning `0x`. Copy the address from the button at the top of your wallet.

**Bridging from another network**

Already holding crypto on BNB Chain, Polygon or similar? Use a bridge to move it to Ethereum first.

## Where your tokens are now

Your balance lives on the presale dashboard at **[presale.ozak.ai](https://presale.ozak.ai)**, under **Total $OZ Bought**. Connect the same wallet you bought with.

:::info Your tokens are not in your wallet yet
That is normal. Presale balances are held on the dashboard and become claimable at the Token Generation Event. See [Can't Find Your $OZ](/guides/find-your-tokens).
