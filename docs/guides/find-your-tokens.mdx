# Can't Find Your $OZ?

Your purchase went through, but your wallet looks empty. This is expected, and your tokens are safe.

## Where your tokens actually are

During the presale, $OZ does not sit in your personal wallet. Your balance is recorded on the presale dashboard.

1. **Check the dashboard.** Go to **[presale.ozak.ai](https://presale.ozak.ai)** and connect the exact wallet you bought with. Your balance is shown there
2. **Wait for the Claim button.** When the presale ends, the Token Generation Event happens and a **Claim** button appears next to your balance
3. **Claim.** Click it and your $OZ is sent to your wallet

:::info Why it works this way
Holding balances on the dashboard until the TGE is standard for presales. It keeps distribution in one auditable place rather than thousands of individual transfers.
:::

## Troubleshooting

### Did my purchase actually go through?

Every Ethereum transaction leaves a receipt you can check yourself.

1. Open your wallet and find the purchase in your activity log
2. Select **View on block explorer**
3. Check the status. **Success** means it went through. **Failed** means it did not

### My transaction failed but my ETH is gone

The network charges a gas fee for the attempt whether it succeeds or fails. If the transaction failed, the gas was spent but your **purchase funds were not** - your ETH, USDC or USDT are still in your wallet. Common causes are insufficient gas or network congestion. Try again.

### Am I connected with the right wallet?

Your balance is tied to the exact address you bought with. If you hold several wallets or accounts, make sure the one connected to [presale.ozak.ai](https://presale.ozak.ai) is the one that made the purchase.
