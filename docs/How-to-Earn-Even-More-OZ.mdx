### Strategy 2: Go Deep - Create Content That Converts

The most successful referrals come from trust. The best way to build trust is by creating content that offers real value.

* YouTube Explainer: Record a simple 5-minute video of your screen. Walk people through the process: "Here’s how I bought my tokens, here's the dashboard, and here's how the referral system works." Put your link in the video description and as a pinned comment.
* Blog Post / Article: Write a detailed post explaining Ozak AI’s AI models or its place in the market. At the end, add a clear call to action with a button that leads to your referral link.
* TikTok & Reels: Create a quick, high-energy 30-second video. Use bold on-screen text to grab attention. Put your link in your bio.

Pro Tip: Most platforms prefer content that keeps users on their site. Post your video or text first, and then add your referral link in the comments or your bio.

In a presale, urgency is your best friend. The price of $OZ increases with each new phase, and you can use this to your advantage.

* Post Before a Price Jump: The Ozak AI dashboard has a countdown timer to the next price increase. Schedule your social media posts to go live 24 hours before that happens to create a natural sense of FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).
