# Contract Audits

Security is the foundation of everything we build. This page carries the official contract address and the results of our independent audits.

## Official contract address

All presale transactions are managed by a single verified smart contract.

```text
0xC73a02c63277fC12F28Ae6D78bD7E1B6b425911B
```

:::danger Verify before you interact
Always confirm you are interacting with this exact address. Trust only the address published on [ozak.ai](https://ozak.ai) and in this documentation. Addresses pasted in DMs, comments or group chats are how funds get stolen.
:::
