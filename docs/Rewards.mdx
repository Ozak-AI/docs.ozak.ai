## **Earn $OZ Tokens Through Quests and Activities**

If you're looking to get involved with Ozak AI without diving straight into the presale, our rewards program is a great way to earn some $OZ tokens just by participating in fun, community-focused activities.

We've set aside a cool $1 million worth of $OZ tokens to reward folks who help spread the word, engage with our platform, and contribute to the vibe.

The rewards program runs until the presale wraps up (we'll announce the end date on our social channels), and rewards are distributed in one go at the Token Generation Event (TGE). No need to be an investor – anyone can join in and rack up points.

## How the Rewards Works

### The OZ Pool

We've pre-allocated $1,000,000 equivalent in $OZ tokens for this rewards program. At TGE, we'll calculate the exact number of tokens based on the market price at that time and divvy them up among participants.

### Earning ozPoints

You'll earn ozPoints by completing various quests and activities. Each activity has a weight (that's the points value), and some can be done multiple times based on frequency limits. The formula is simple:

**ozPoints = Sum of (Activity Weight × Number of Times Performed)**

We track everything through our platform, so make sure you're logged in and connected where needed. Points show up in your dashboard as you complete tasks.

### Payout Calculation

Your share of the $1M pool is proportional to your ozPoints compared to everyone else's. Here's the math:

**Your $OZ Allocation = (Your ozPoints ÷ Total ozPoints from All Users) × $1,000,000 worth of $OZ**

For example, if the total ozPoints across all participants is 100, and you've got 1 point, you'd snag 1% of the pool – that's $10,000 worth of $OZ tokens at TGE prices. The more points you earn, the bigger your slice!

### Distribution

Everything gets distributed in one batch right after the campaign ends, at TGE. You'll claim your tokens through the dashboard, just like presale buyers. Keep an eye on @OzakAGI on X and Telegram for updates.

## Getting Started
