#### Do I need to buy tokens myself before I can share my referral link?

No, you can generate your link and start sharing right away without making a purchase.

#### Is the 10% bonus paid in cash or tokens?

All bonuses are paid in $OZ tokens, which are added directly to your presale dashboard and will be claimable along with any tokens you purchased.
