* All cliffs & vesting handled by audited smart contracts; no manual unlocks.

### Deflation & Buy-Back Policy

* Currently no automatic burn.
* However, the treasury can  allocate surplus revenues to periodic market buy-backs or fee-burn mechanics once the platform reaches cash-flow positive status.

### Liquidity Strategy

* 10 % LP reserve (non-circulating) is deployed across at least one tier-1 CEX and Uniswap v3 on launch week.
* Liquidity tokens are timelocked for 12 months to assure traders of depth and price stability.

### Economic Alignment at a Glance

* Users stake $OZ → earn fee rebates & vote.
* Builders receive ecosystem grants → expand predictive modules → grow fee volume.
* Validators restake $OZ → secure data integrity → earn restaking yield.
* Token value accrues via fee sink + scarcity once emissions end.
