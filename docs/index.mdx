## Why we built it

If you have ever looked at the markets and felt like you were drinking from a firehose, you are not alone. Millions of data points, headlines and posts move prices every second, and keeping up is more than a full-time job.

<p className="oz-lead">So we asked a simple question: what if you had a tireless, genuinely smart AI partner working around the clock, sifting the noise for the things that actually matter?</p>

That is Ozak AI. The goal is to put the kind of predictive tooling once reserved for large trading desks in everyone's hands, without needing a team of data scientists to run it.
