:::tip Neon Green is an accent
`#A6FF00` carries the brand because it is used sparingly. On large fills it is hard to read and loses its effect. Keep it to accents, and check contrast when putting text on it - as text on white it fails, so pair it with black instead.
:::

## Usage checklist

- [ ] Correct logo variant for the background - dark logo on light, light logo on dark
- [ ] Logo unmodified, with clear space around it
- [ ] Neon Green used as an accent, not a background fill
- [ ] Sequel Sans for both headings and body
- [ ] Contrast checked for readability

Questions, or need an asset that is not here? Email **support@ozak.ai**.
