### Usage Guidelines: The Do's and Don'ts

To maintain brand consistency, please follow these simple guidelines when using our assets.

| Do                                                      | Don't                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Use the official, unmodified logos provided.            | Distort, stretch, or change the color of the logo.                       |
| Ensure there is enough clear space around the logo.     | Place the logo on a busy or cluttered background.                        |
| Use the white logo on dark backgrounds for readability. | Use the primary logo on a background that doesn't offer enough contrast. |
| Adhere to the official color palette.                   | Introduce new colors that are not part of the brand palette.             |

Thank you for helping us build a strong and consistent brand!
