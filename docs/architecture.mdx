## The infrastructure underneath

Ozak AI is designed to run on distributed infrastructure rather than a single company's servers - the direction the industry calls DePIN, or decentralised physical infrastructure.

The aim is a platform that is more resilient and less dependent on any single point of failure. This is the direction we are building in, not a finished state.
