## **The $OZ Token: Fuel for the AI Engine**

Meet $OZ, the native utility token of the Ozak AI ecosystem.

Think of $OZ not just as a cryptocurrency, but as the key that unlocks the platform's full potential. It’s the fuel, the access pass, and the mechanism that ensures everyone involved - from users to builders - is working toward the same goal. It’s the economic heart of everything we do.

### More Than a Coin: What $OZ Actually Does 

The most important question about any token is, "What can I actually do with it?" With $OZ, the answer is plenty. It’s woven into every aspect of the platform.

**Pay for Platform Use**: Just like you need gas to drive a car, you use $OZ to power the AI. Small fees in $OZ are used to run your Prediction Agents, store data in your private Data Vault, and automate your strategies.

**Put Your Tokens to Work (Staking)**: You can lock up your $OZ tokens to help secure the network and validate the predictions it makes. In return for your contribution, you earn a share of the protocol's fees and other rewards. It’s a way to make your investment productive.

**Get Rewarded for Helping Out**: The ecosystem fund, paid in $OZ, is there to encourage community growth. This includes grants for developers building new tools, prizes for trading contests, and rewards for providing liquidity.

**Unlock Fee Discounts**: The more $OZ you stake, the more you save. Users in higher tiers will get reduced fees, making the platform even more efficient for our most committed community members.

### A Fair Launch: How $OZ is Distributed

We believe a strong community is built on a fair foundation. That’s why the tokenomics of $OZ are designed for long-term health and decentralization, not short-term hype.

First, the basics: there is a **fixed supply of 10 billion $OZ**. No more will ever be created, ensuring the token is designed to be scarce.

Here’s how that supply is allocated:

| **Bucket**                | **% of Supply** | **Cliff** | **Vesting**                  | **Purpose**                                                                |
| ------------------------- | --------------- | --------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Presale**               | 30%             | 1 month   | 6 months (10% liquid at TGE) | To get the token into the hands of our earliest believers and community.   |
| **Ecosystem & Community** | 30%             | 3 months  | 18 months                    | To fund grants, rewards, and contests that help the platform grow.         |
| **Future Reserve**        | 20%             | 3 months  | 18 months                    | Kept for strategic partnerships, acquisitions, and long-term planning.     |
| **Team**                  | 10%             | 3 months  | 24 months                    | To align our core builders with the long-term success of the project.      |
| **Liquidity & Listings**  | 10%             | 0 months  | 0 months                     | To make sure there's a healthy market for buying and selling on exchanges. |

Note: 10% of Presale tokens are unlocked at the Token Generation Event (TGE) to provide initial circulation.

**Cliff**: Think of this as a waiting period. It's a set amount of time after the token launch before any tokens from that bucket can begin to be released. For example, the Team has a 3-month cliff, meaning no team tokens are unlocked for the first three months, ensuring they are committed to the project's initial growth.

**Vesting**: This is the gradual release schedule that happens after the cliff period is over. Instead of getting all their tokens at once, recipients get them slowly, bit by bit, over the vesting period. For instance, the Team's tokens are released in small, equal amounts over 24 months.

### What This All Means in Plain English:

**No Big Team Dumps**: Notice the Team and Reserve tokens have long "vesting" periods. This means the team has to wait months before they can even begin to access their tokens, and they receive them slowly over two years. This ensures we're all focused on long-term growth.

**A Healthy Market from Day One**: A full 10% of the supply is dedicated to providing liquidity on exchanges right at launch. This helps create a stable and reliable market for everyone.

**Trust Through Code**: This entire release schedule isn't based on a promise; it's locked into audited smart contracts. The process is automated and transparent, removing the risk of manual manipulation.

### An Honest Look: Risks & Our Plan

Every project has risks. Building trust means being upfront about them and having a plan in place. Here’s what we’ve thought about and how we’re prepared.

**The Risk**: Big sell-offs when tokens are unlocked.

**Our Mitigation**: Instead of releasing tokens in big chunks, our unlocks are gradual and linear, happening every month. This, combined with deep liquidity from day one, helps the market absorb new supply smoothly.

**The Risk**: The AI models don't perform as expected over time.

**Our Mitigation**: That's exactly what the huge 30% Ecosystem fund is for. It gives us the resources to incentivize the world's best data scientists and developers to constantly build, test, and optimize our Predictive Agents.

**The Risk**: Hacks or smart contract bugs.

**Our Mitigation**: Our contracts undergo full security audits from reputable firms before launch. Additionally, by using established technologies for staking, we add another powerful layer of economic security to the system.

Ultimately, the entire $OZ economy is designed to create a positive feedback loop: users stake $OZ to get value, builders get grants to create more tools, the tools generate more fees, and the token captures that value. When the platform wins, the entire community wins with it.
