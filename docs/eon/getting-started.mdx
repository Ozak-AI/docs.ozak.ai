## Security basics

Eon is **non-custodial**. We never hold your funds, portfolio analysis is read-only, and every trade goes through your own wallet with your approval.

:::caution
**We will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys**, and we will never send you a dashboard link by DM. Full detail: [Security & Trust](/security).
:::
