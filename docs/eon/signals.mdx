## Every signal shows its work

You are never asked to trust a verdict blind. Each card carries:

<div className="oz-anatomy">
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Signal</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">The name, and the asset it fired on</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Pattern</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">The specific setup behind it</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Data</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">The underlying numbers that triggered it</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Explanation</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">Plain English, not jargon</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Conviction</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">A percentage, not a yes or no</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Verdict</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">Strong Sell through to Strong Buy</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Age</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">How long ago it fired</span></div>
  <div className="oz-anatomy__row"><span className="oz-anatomy__k">Confluence</span><span className="oz-anatomy__v">What else agrees, listed as tags</span></div>
</div>

:::note We publish our record
Once a prediction market window closes, the card shows how the call resolved. Every prediction is published, including the ones that did not work out. Knowing the record is part of knowing how much weight to give the next call. Nothing here is a guarantee.
:::
