# Home: Asking Eon Anything

Home is a prompt box, not a dashboard. Type your question the way you would say it out loud.

Two selectors sit beneath the box: a **scope**, for example global crypto, and a **coin**, if you want the question pointed at one asset.

## Questions that work well

<div className="oz-prompts">
  <p className="oz-prompt">Is Bitcoin currently in a bullish or bearish phase based on recent market structure?</p>
  <p className="oz-prompt">Analyze BTC using RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands for short-term momentum direction.</p>
  <p className="oz-prompt">What does it mean if ETH's 9-day EMA crosses below the 50-day EMA while volume is declining?</p>
  <p className="oz-prompt">Identify key support and resistance levels for ADA USDT using the Donchian Channel and ATR.</p>
</div>

## What an answer looks like

Eon does not return a wall of numbers. A typical response reads like an analyst note:

<div className="oz-steps oz-steps--compact">
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">1</span><span className="oz-steps__desc"><strong>Current status</strong> - the one-line read</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">2</span><span className="oz-steps__desc"><strong>Price structure and momentum</strong> - where price sits and the levels either side</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">3</span><span className="oz-steps__desc"><strong>Volume and market structure</strong> - including whether volatility is compressing</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">4</span><span className="oz-steps__desc"><strong>Broader context</strong> - recent developments and news sentiment</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">5</span><span className="oz-steps__desc"><strong>Assessment</strong> - where it commits to a view</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">6</span><span className="oz-steps__desc"><strong>Key levels to watch</strong> - the prices that would confirm a move either way</span></div>
</div>

Every answer carries its **sources** so you can check the work, plus copy and feedback controls. Below it, Eon suggests follow-up questions, and the conversation stays open.

:::tip It will tell you to wait
If the market is consolidating and there is no clean setup, Eon says so rather than inventing a trade. A tool that only ever finds reasons to trade is not being straight with you.
:::

Every conversation is saved to [Threads](/eon/threads).
