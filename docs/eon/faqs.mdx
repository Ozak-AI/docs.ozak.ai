# Eon FAQs & Troubleshooting

Custody, accuracy, credits, wallets, and the things that go wrong.

<p className="oz-sectionlabel">Using Eon</p>

<div className="oz-faq">
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Does Eon hold my funds?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">No. Eon is non-custodial. Portfolio analysis is read-only, and every trade goes through your own wallet with your approval.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Is Eon accurate?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">Eon publishes its own track record. Under <a href="/eon/signals">Signals &rarr; Prediction Markets</a> you can see how every resolved call turned out. Nothing in Eon is a guarantee, and every signal arrives with a conviction level and its reasoning so you can weigh it yourself.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Will Eon trade for me automatically?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">No. Eon surfaces analysis, signals and setups. You decide what to act on and you authorise every trade.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">What does a query cost?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">It depends on depth. A full analysis costs more than a quick lookup, and you are warned before anything expensive runs.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Do I need Premium?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">No. Free includes the AI agents, discovery, most of the research suite, the screener, one-wallet portfolio analysis and spot trading. Premium adds Signals, the advanced research pages, perps and prediction markets, and alerts.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Where do prediction market odds come from?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">Polymarket.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Can I buy credits without subscribing?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">Yes, instantly.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Do I need a wallet?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">You can sign in with Google or email, but a Web3 wallet unlocks portfolio analysis and trading.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oz-faq__row">
    <p className="oz-faq__q">Is there a mobile app?</p>
    <p className="oz-faq__a">Eon is fully responsive in a mobile browser. Nothing to download.</p>
  </div>
</div>
