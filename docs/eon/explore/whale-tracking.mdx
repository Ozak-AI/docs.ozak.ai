# Whale Tracking

Track large wallet moves and whale transactions across the market in real time, per token.

## The AI analysis

- **Volume and activity** - total whale volume and transaction count, with an accumulation or distribution read
- **Blockchain distribution** - where whale activity is concentrated
- **Exchange flows** - deposits versus withdrawals and the net figure
- **Concentration snapshot** - largest and average transaction size
- **Key market signal** - the headline takeaway

Three quick verdicts sit underneath: **Interpretation**, **Bias** with a confidence level, and **Risk**.

## Live Whale Alerts

A live stream showing chain, asset, amount, value and the route between named entities where known. Verifiable, not just a number on a screen.
