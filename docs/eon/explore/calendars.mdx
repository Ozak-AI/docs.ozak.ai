# Event & Economic Calendars

Two calendars, one for crypto-native events and one for macro.

## Event Calendar

Market-moving project events in one place: announcements, listings, mainnet migrations, livestreams, competitions and more.

Filter by **Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow, This Week** or **Next Week**, or pick a date.

## Economic Calendar

Macro releases with actuals and forecasts, searchable by **event title** or **country**.

Each entry carries an **impact rating** of low, medium or high, so you can see at a glance what actually matters. Coverage spans major currencies and the releases that move them: manufacturing activity, retail sales, government budgets, confidence indices, trade data and more.

:::tip Why this matters for crypto
Crypto does not trade in a vacuum. Knowing when a major print lands is often the difference between holding through volatility and being surprised by it.
:::
