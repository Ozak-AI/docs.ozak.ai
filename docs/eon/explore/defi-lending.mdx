# DeFi Lending

Track lending rates, collateral and liquidity across **Aave V2 and V3**. Requires an Eon Premium subscription.

## What is on the page

- **Protocol Health** - a composite score with liquidity depth and reserve coverage
- **7 Day Rate Forecast** - projected supply APY and borrow rate ranges, with a confidence level and a stated method
- **Utilization Zone** - a gauge with low and optimal bands
- **Full metrics** - utilisation, supply APY, variable borrow, spread, TVL, borrowed, available, reserves
- **Deeper tabs** - Rate Metrics, Liquidity & Utilization, Protocol Metrics, Cross-Protocol Comparison

The AI here gives direct guidance, including telling you when a market is not worth engaging with.
