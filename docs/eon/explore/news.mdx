# News & Sentiment Analysis

Every headline that moves the market, filtered and aggregated with real-time sentiment.

## What is on the page

- **Coin chart** - news volume broken down by asset and sentiment
- **Market chart** - news volume over time
- **All news** feed, filterable by **Topic, Sentiment** or **Coin**

## Why it is different

Each story comes with an AI summary that states the implication, not just the facts, plus its source and topic tag.

A wallet exploit is not filed as "security news." It comes with a read on the likely effect on volatility and confidence. That is the difference between being informed and being ready.
