# Economics

Macro liquidity for crypto, across four views: **Stablecoins, Correlation, Sectors** and **NVT Ratio**. Requires an Eon Premium subscription.

## Stablecoins

The one to watch. It tracks total stablecoin supply, issuer dominance, weekly momentum and the share sitting on exchanges ready to deploy.

<p className="oz-lead">Rising supply means capital entering the market, falling supply means capital leaving. Watching it tells you whether there is fuel behind a move.</p>

AI Insights breaks down into Overview, Liquidity Signal, Flow Analysis and Signals, with an issuer-by-issuer breakdown underneath.
