## Available indicators

| Indicator | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Market Cap | Overall size and how it is trending |
| Circulating Supply vs Total Supply | How much is yet to enter circulation |
| Top Holder Concentration | Whether ownership is dangerously concentrated |
| On-chain Transaction Volume | Whether the network is actually being used |
