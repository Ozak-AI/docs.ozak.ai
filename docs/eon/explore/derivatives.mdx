Each carries an explainer, so you do not need an options background to read the page.

## Risk warnings

A banner escalates when conditions get dangerous, and it does not hide behind jargon. When multiple signals breach, it tells you plainly to reduce leverage and explains why.

AI summaries are **scored**, so you can see at a glance whether liquidation risk or market activity is the thing to watch.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Overview | Scored liquidation risk and market turnover |
| Leverage Cost | Funding converted into an annualised carry cost, predicted versus actual funding, and funding broken out by exchange with each venue's share of open interest |
| Market Fragility | The **Cascade Score**, a measure of cascading liquidation risk. Elevated readings historically coincide with higher flash-crash probability |
| Options Market | The full volatility smile: implied versus realised vol, risk reversal, butterfly and skew slope, with positioning guidance |
| Volatility Premium | Vol carry premium and the realised volatility term structure across 7, 14 and 30 days |
