## Customising your view

Use **Columns** to choose what you see and **Filters** to layer extra criteria. Default columns cover price, 24 hour change, market cap, MC/FDV, 7 day change, distance from all-time high and volume.

Every row has a **Buy / Sell** button, so screening flows straight into acting.
