## What you get back

**Portfolio Value** - your total, annualised change, a value chart across 1M to 1Y, and a **drawdown curve** underneath.

**Quick Stats** - annualised return, Sharpe ratio, win rate, max drawdown, volatility and profit factor.

**Portfolio Composition** - every holding by value and share.

## Detailed metrics

**Overview** - annualised and total return, **Sharpe ratio** (above 1 is a strong risk-adjusted return), **Sortino ratio** (downside risk-adjusted), **Calmar ratio** (return over max drawdown) and annualised volatility. Every metric has an explainer.

**Composition** - an **HHI score** and a concentration gauge running from diversified to highly concentrated, a performance versus risk-adjusted toggle, automatic category tagging, a full asset table with an **Analyze** button on every holding, and a chain-by-chain breakdown.

**DeFi Protocols · Risk & Drawdown · Win / Loss** - deeper detail on positions, drawdown history and hit rate.

At the bottom, an **Ask me anything about your portfolio** box lets you question any of it.
