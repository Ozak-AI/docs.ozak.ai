# Discover

Discover is for browsing rather than searching.

At the top, Eon surfaces **AI-flagged opportunities**, each with a read on the setup, a tag such as Oversold, and a Trade button.

Below that, an asset table across **Watchlist, Spot, Perpetuals and Cross Market**. Every row carries an inline AI verdict, so you are reading an opinionated list rather than a neutral one.

## Filters

| Filter | What it selects |
|---|---|
| Trending Now | Strong momentum with high volume |
| Top Gainers | Best 24 hour performers |
| Recovery Zone | Deep drawdowns with bounce potential |
| DeFi Leaders | Top DeFi tokens |
| AI Revolution | AI and machine learning tokens |
| Layer 1 Chains | Mainnet and L1 blockchains |
| Meme King | Meme and community tokens |
| Max Volume | High trading activity relative to market cap |
| Strong Buy | Assets currently rated Strong Buy |

Assets are badged by category, and the star icon adds anything to your watchlist.
