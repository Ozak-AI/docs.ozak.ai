# Threads

You cannot keep everything in your head. As you use Eon you generate a lot of insight: chart setups, token deep dives, market summaries.

Every conversation is saved automatically.

Open **Threads** from the sidebar to find any past analysis, or start a fresh one with **New Thread**. Threads are titled by their opening question, so finding the one you want is quick.
