Token-to-token swaps routed through **aggregated DEX liquidity** to find the best available price.

## Perpetuals

<p className="oz-premium">Premium feature</p>

**Market data:** index price, 24 hour change and volume, open interest, funding rate, and leverage up to **40x**.

**Chart:** candlesticks from 1 minute to 1 day, with mark and index overlays.

**Placing an order:**

<div className="oz-steps oz-steps--compact">
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">1</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Select the market, for example BTC-PERP</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">2</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Choose margin mode and leverage</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">3</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Pick <strong>Buy / Long</strong> or <strong>Sell / Short</strong></span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">4</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Enter size, or use the 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% shortcuts</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">5</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Set your price, or take the mid</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">6</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Choose your order type</span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">7</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Optionally attach <strong>take profit</strong> and <strong>stop loss</strong></span></div>
  <div className="oz-steps__row"><span className="oz-steps__num">8</span><span className="oz-steps__desc">Set <strong>reduce only</strong> and <strong>time in force</strong> if needed</span></div>

Track everything under **Order Book, Positions, Open Orders** and **Trade History**, and manage collateral with deposit and withdraw.

There is also an **AI Analysis** button on the trading screen, so you can get a read without leaving your order ticket.
