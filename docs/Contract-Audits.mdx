### Independent Security Audits

To ensure the integrity of our platform, we have engaged leading third-party security firms to conduct in-depth audits of our smart contracts. These audits involve a line-by-line review of our code to identify potential vulnerabilities before they can be exploited.

Here are the results of our audits:

#### Audited by CertiK

Audit Completed: June 16, 2025

Key Findings:

* No critical or major vulnerabilities were found.
* All medium and minor issues identified were promptly acknowledged and resolved by our development team.
* Additional security enhancements were implemented, including improved address validations and protection against reinitialization.
* A secure multisig wallet is in place for managing funds, requiring multiple team members to approve transactions.
