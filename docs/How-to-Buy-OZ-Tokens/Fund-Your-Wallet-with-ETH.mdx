---
title: Fund Your Wallet with ETH
---

To participate in the Ozak presale, you’ll need Ethereum (ETH) in your personal wallet. ETH is used to buy $OZ tokens and cover gas fees on the Ethereum network.

***

### 1. Buy ETH with a Card

You can purchase ETH using a debit or credit card through trusted on-ramp services. After buying, transfer the ETH to your wallet address.

Note: Exchange wallets like Binance, Coinbase, or Kraken will not work. You must use a self-custody wallet such as MetaMask or Trust Wallet.

Popular platforms include:

* [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/) – Great for beginners. Supports card payments and bank transfers
* [Binance](https://www.binance.com/) – Offers card payments and low fees
* [Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/) – Mobile-friendly with easy onboarding
* [Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/) – Secure and ideal for users wanting tutorials
* [MoonPay](https://www.moonpay.com/) – No exchange signup required; simple ETH purchase experience

After purchase, send your ETH to your wallet’s Ethereum address (starts with 0x...).

***

### 2. Bridge Crypto to Ethereum

Already holding crypto on another chain like BNB, Polygon, or Avalanche? You can bridge it to Ethereum.

Bridge options:

* [Binance Bridge](https://www.binance.com/)
* [Multichain](https://app.multichain.org/)
* [Portal Bridge](https://portalbridge.com/)
* [Hop Protocol](https://app.hop.exchange/)

Bridging Steps:

1. Go to a bridge site and connect your wallet
2. Choose the source chain and destination (Ethereum)
3. Input the amount and confirm the transaction
4. Wait for the transfer to complete (a few minutes)

***

### 3. Secure Your ETH

Once ETH is in your wallet, make sure it stays safe.

Recommended wallets:

* [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/)
* [Trust Wallet](https://trustwallet.com/)
* [Ledger](https://www.ledger.com/) (hardware wallet, optional)

***

### 4. Use ETH to Join the Ozak Presale

With ETH in your wallet, visit the official presale page:
[https://ozak.ai/presale](https://ozak.ai/presale)

* Connect your wallet
* Select ETH as your payment method
* Confirm the transaction and receive $OZ
