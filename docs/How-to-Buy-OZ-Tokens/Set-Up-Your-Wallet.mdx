---
title: Set Up Your Wallet
---

Setting up a cryptocurrency wallet is the first step to managing your assets and accessing decentralized platforms like Ozak AI.

<Admonition type="tip" title="Important">
  Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone. Store them safely offline.
</Admonition>

* Install [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/) (browser/mobile) or [Trust Wallet](https://trustwallet.com/) (iOS/Android), or your preferred ERC-20 wallet.
* Create or import your wallet; back up the seed phrase offline.
* Confirm the network dropdown shows Ethereum Mainnet.

***

### 1. Create a Wallet with MetaMask (Browser)

MetaMask is a browser extension that lets you manage Ethereum-based tokens like $OZ from your desktop.

#### Steps:

Install MetaMask

* Visit [https://metamask.io](https://metamask.io/)
* Download the extension for your browser (Chrome, Brave, Firefox, or Edge)

Set Up Your Wallet

* Click Get Started and choose Create a Wallet
* Create a secure password
* Back up your Secret Recovery Phrase and store it offline. This is the only way to restore your wallet if you lose access

Start Using MetaMask

* Your wallet is now ready
* Make sure it’s set to the Ethereum Mainnet
* You can now receive ETH and interact with dApps like [ozak.ai/presale](https://ozak.ai/presale)

***

### 2. Create a Wallet with Trust Wallet (Mobile)

Trust Wallet is a mobile-first app that supports Ethereum and other major blockchains. It’s ideal if you prefer managing crypto from your phone.

#### Steps:

Download the App

* Go to [https://trustwallet.com](https://trustwallet.com/)
* Or search “Trust Wallet” on the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android)

Set Up Your Wallet

* Open the app and tap Create a New Wallet
* Agree to the Terms of Service

Secure Your Recovery Phrase

* Write down your 12-word recovery phrase and keep it offline
* This is the only way to recover your wallet

Start Using Trust Wallet

* Make sure you're set to the Ethereum network
* Add ETH by buying directly in the app or transferring from an exchange
* You’re now ready to connect your wallet at [ozak.ai/presale](https://ozak.ai/presale) and purchase $OZ tokens
