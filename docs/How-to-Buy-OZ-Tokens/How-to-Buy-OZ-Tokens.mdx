### **Before You Start**

| **✅ Do**                                                                                     | **❌ Don’t**                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Use a **self-custody wallet** (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or any ERC-20-compatible alternative) | Send from an exchange wallet (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken).        |
| Keep at least **$10–$15 in ETH** for gas.                                                    | Forget gas fees - your buy will fail if the wallet has 0 ETH.    |
| Double-check you are on the **Ethereum (ERC-20) network**.                                   | Switch networks after connecting - this breaks the presale dApp. |

MetaMask and Trust Wallet are the recommended options because they’re fully tested with the Ozak dApp, but any self-custody wallet that supports Ethereum Mainnet will work.

### **Step 1 – Set Up Your Wallet**

* Install **[MetaMask](https://metamask.io/)**[ ](https://metamask.io/)(browser/mobile) or **[Trust Wallet](https://trustwallet.com/)** (iOS/Android), or your preferred ERC-20 wallet.
* Create or import your wallet; back up the seed phrase offline.
* Confirm the **network dropdown** shows **Ethereum Mainnet**.

![](/img/20d84994-5a39-80f3-a488-fb213a025fb8.png)

### **Step 2 – Fund the Wallet with ETH**

You need enough ETH (or USDT/USDC on Ethereum) to cover the token purchase plus gas fees.

The two most common ways:

1. Centralised exchange → withdraw

* Buy ETH or stablecoins on [Binance](https://www.binance.com/), [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/), [Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/), etc.
* Select the ERC-20 network when withdrawing.
* Paste your wallet’s `0x…` address and send. Funds usually land within a few minutes.

1. Fiat on-ramp inside the wallet

* MetaMask and Trust Wallet integrate services like [Ramp](https://ramp.network/), [Transak](https://transak.com/), and [MoonPay](https://www.moonpay.com/).
* Purchase ETH with a debit/credit card and have it delivered straight to your wallet.
* Allow for card KYC and a slightly higher fee, but it’s the fastest route if you don’t yet hold crypto.

Rule of thumb: Load at least $50 USD worth of ETH if you’re new - $35+ for tokens, $10-15 for gas.

Now, open your wallet and verify your ETH (or USDT/USDC) balance.

Still on Ethereum Mainnet? Good - time to buy $OZ.

### **Step 3 – Open the Presale Dashboard**

* Go to app.**ozak.ai**.
* Click **“Connect Wallet.”** Choose MetaMask or Trust Wallet, or the wallet that you’re using.

  ![](/img/20d84994-5a39-80f0-af08-d3f4629a9b7a.png)
* Approve the connection pop-up in your wallet.

  ![](/img/20e84994-5a39-8075-a0df-da94ca48ac48.jpg)

### **Step 4 – Choose Your Payment Token**

Supported currencies:

* ETH
* USDT (ERC-20)
* USDC (ERC-20)

Pick the token you funded in the previous step. ETH chosen here for example.

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-80a3-a0d1-c28d12e89e1f.jpg)

### **Step 5 – Enter an Amount**

* Type how much you want to spend (e.g., **0.25 ETH** or **300 USDT**).

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-802e-b9ad-d2fffac393a3.jpg)

* The dashboard instantly shows **the number of $OZ you’ll receive**.
* Click **“Buy”**.

### **Step 6 – Approve & Confirm**

* Your wallet pops up with the transaction details (gas + amount).

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-8099-a7ff-d1b8aea7964d.jpg)

* Review them, then click Confirm.
* USDT/USDC users: you’ll see two prompts - first Approve, then Buy.

Wait for one network confirmation (≈ 15 sec). The dApp will show a success banner.

![](/img/187c837b-39d9-4e40-a87d-ce6a8816e6ed.png)

### **Step 7 – You’re Done**

* Log in to app.ozak.ai/dashboard. Under “Total $OZ Bought” you’ll see the tokens you just purchased.

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-8047-b456-ed5ae428b40a.jpg)

### **Troubleshooting & Safety Tips**

* **Transaction “Stuck Pending”** → Speed it up in MetaMask by adding +10 Gwei.
* **Wrong Network Error** → Switch MetaMask back to **Ethereum Mainnet** and refresh.
* Always verify the URL starts with **[https://ozak.ai](https://ozak.ai/)** - bookmark it to dodge phishing sites.
* Support is *only* by email at [support@ozak.ai](mailto\:support@ozak.ai). Anyone from Ozak AI will never reach out to you first.
