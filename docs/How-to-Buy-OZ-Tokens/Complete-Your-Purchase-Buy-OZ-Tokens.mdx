### Step 1 – Open the Presale Dashboard

* Go to[ **app.ozak.ai**.](https://app.ozak.ai/)
* Click **“Connect Wallet.”** Choose MetaMask or Trust Wallet, or the wallet that you’re using.

  ![](/img/20d84994-5a39-80f0-af08-d3f4629a9b7a.png)
* Approve the connection pop-up in your wallet.

  ![](/img/20e84994-5a39-8075-a0df-da94ca48ac48.jpg)

### Step 2 – Choose Your Payment Token

Supported currencies:

* ETH
* USDT (ERC-20)
* USDC (ERC-20)

Pick the token you funded in the previous step. ETH chosen here for example.

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-80a3-a0d1-c28d12e89e1f.jpg)

### Step 3 – Enter an Amount

* Type how much you want to spend (e.g., **0.25 ETH** or **300 USDT**).

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-802e-b9ad-d2fffac393a3.jpg)

* The dashboard instantly shows **the number of $OZ you’ll receive**.
* Click **“Buy”**.

### Step 4 – Approve & Confirm

* Your wallet pops up with the transaction details (gas + amount).

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-8099-a7ff-d1b8aea7964d.jpg)

* Review them, then click Confirm.
* USDT/USDC users: you’ll see two prompts - first Approve, then Buy.

Wait for one network confirmation (≈ 15 sec). The dApp will show a success banner.

![](/img/187c837b-39d9-4e40-a87d-ce6a8816e6ed.png)

### Step 5 – You’re Done

* Log in to app.ozak.ai/dashboard. Under “Total $OZ Bought” you’ll see the tokens you just purchased.

![](/img/20e84994-5a39-8047-b456-ed5ae428b40a.jpg)

* They remain locked until the presale ends, at which point a Claim button will appear right here.
* Follow [@OzakAGI](https://x.com/OzakAGI) on X and the official [Telegram](https://t.me/OzakAGI) for the claim-date announcement.

### Troubleshooting & Safety Tips

* **Transaction “Stuck Pending”** → Speed it up in MetaMask by adding +10 Gwei.
* **Wrong Network Error** → Switch MetaMask back to **Ethereum Mainnet** and refresh.
* Always verify the URL starts with **[https://ozak.ai](https://ozak.ai/)** - bookmark it to dodge phishing sites.
* Support is *only* by email at [support@ozak.ai](mailto\:support@ozak.ai). Anyone from Ozak AI will never reach out to you first.

### What Happens After the Presale?

* A snapshot block is taken; final allocations are fixed.
* Claim button appears on your Ozak dashboard - single click, pay gas, receive OZ tokens.
* DEX listings follow; exact block number announced on social channels.
