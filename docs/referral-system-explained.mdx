Ozak AI Referral & Ranking System


_Share → Earn 10% → Rank up → Hold $100+ → Enter $1M draw._


Community-driven growth sits at the heart of Ozak AI. That’s why Ozak AI’s presale is more than a chance to buy $OZ at an early-bird price - it’s a community game that rewards you for spreading the word and stacking tokens.


Ozak AI’s referral program is open to everyone participating in the $OZ token presale. When you join the presale, you get a unique referral code. Share this code with friends, family, or your social media followers. For every person who uses your code to buy $OZ tokens, you receive a 10% bonus in $OZ tokens based on their purchase amount. For example, if someone you refer buys 1,000 $OZ tokens, you get an extra 100 $OZ tokens as a reward.


There’s no cap on how many people you can refer or how much you can earn - your earning potential is truly unlimited. The more you share, the more you stand to gain. All bonuses are credited automatically to your wallet once the referred purchase is complete. This system is designed to help Ozak AI’s community grow organically, with every member having the chance to benefit directly from their outreach efforts.


Three mechanics work together:

1. Referral Bonus – Earn extra $OZ by inviting friends.
2. Ranking Ladder – Unlock status perks as your total spend grows.
3. $1 Million Giveaway – A giant prize pool reserved for holders who keep at least $100 worth of $OZ.

Frequently Asked Questions


Do I need to buy tokens before sharing my link?


No. Anyone with a connected wallet can generate and share a link—even Omegas.


Is the 10% bonus paid in cash or tokens?


In $OZ tokens, delivered to your wallet once the presale ends.


Can I create multiple wallets to game the system?


Splitting purchases dilutes your rank and may trigger anti-sybil checks. Keep everything in one wallet.


What if my friend forgets to use my link?


They must open your URL _before_ connecting their wallet. Ask them to reconnect via your link.


Is KYC required to enter the $1 M giveaway?


No. Eligibility is on-chain; local tax reporting remains your responsibility.
