**10% of presale tokens unlock at the Token Generation Event** to provide initial circulation.

### Cliff and vesting, in plain terms

**Cliff** is a waiting period after launch before any tokens from a bucket start releasing. The team has a 3-month cliff, so no team tokens move for the first three months.

**Vesting** is the gradual release after the cliff. Rather than arriving at once, tokens release steadily across the period. Team tokens release in equal monthly amounts over 24 months.

## Risks, and what we do about them

Every project carries risk. These are the ones we plan around.

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Large sell pressure at unlocks | Unlocks are gradual and linear month by month rather than in chunks, and launch liquidity helps the market absorb new supply |
| AI models underperforming over time | The 30% ecosystem allocation exists to fund data scientists and developers to keep building, testing and improving the models |
| Smart contract bugs or exploits | Contracts undergo independent third-party audits before launch. See [Contract Audits](/audits) |

The intent is a loop rather than a one-way extraction: holders provide capital and participation, builders receive grants to create tools, those tools generate fees, and the token captures that value.
