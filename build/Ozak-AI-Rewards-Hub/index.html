Ozak AI Rewards Hub

Want to get involved with the Ozak AI ecosystem and earn some $OZ tokens without diving straight into the presale? You’re in the right place!

Our Rewards Hub is a community-focused program designed to thank you for helping us grow. We've set aside a $1 million pool of $OZ tokens for community members who complete fun quests and activities. No investment is necessary - this is all about participation.

How It Works: Earn XPs​

The system is designed to be simple and transparent. Think of it as a game where you complete missions to earn points, and your points determine your share of the prize.

  1. The Prize Pool: A total of $1,000,000 worth of $OZ is waiting to be claimed.
  2. The Game: You complete quests to earn XPs. Each quest has a different point value, and some can be done daily.
  3. The Payout: Your final reward is based on your share of the total XPs earned by the entire community.
  4. Simple Example: If all participants combined earn 100,000 XPs, and you earned 1,000 of them (that's 1% of the total), you would receive 1% of the prize pool - a cool $10,000 worth of $OZ tokens!

All rewards are calculated and distributed at the end of the presale during the Token Generation Event (TGE).

Your 5-Step Quick-Start Guide​

Getting started is easy. Follow these steps to begin earning today:

  1. Connect Your Wallet: Head over to app.ozak.ai and connect your ERC-20 wallet (like MetaMask) so we can track your points and you can claim your rewards later.
  2. Check the Rewards Hub: Your rewards hub dashboard will show your current XPs and a list of available quests.
  3. Complete Quests: Pick an activity and follow the instructions.
  4. Track Your Progress: Log in daily to complete new quests and watch your points balance grow!

Pro Tips to Maximize Your Earnings​

  1. Be Consistent: The daily quests are easy points. They add up quickly over time, so make them part of your daily routine.
  2. Go for High-Value Quests: A single well-written Reddit post or X thread can earn you a massive point boost. Set aside some time to create quality content.
  3. Stay Active: We'll be adding new weekly challenges and campaigns. Keep checking the dashboard so you don't miss out.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)​

When does the rewards program end?​

This particular phase of program runs until the presale concludes, right before the TGE. We'll announce the official end date on our social channels.

Do I need to buy or hold $OZ to participate?​

Nope! This program is open to everyone, whether you're an investor or just a community supporter.

How do I know my XPs were counted?​

You can see your updated balance in real-time on your rewards dashboard for most activities.

Can I do the same task multiple times?​

Yes, but only according to the frequency limits listed in the quest table (e.g., daily, weekly). This keeps it fair for everyone.
