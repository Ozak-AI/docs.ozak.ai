How to Buy $OZ

Buying in a presale looks technical the first time. It is not. This walks you through the whole thing.

Only one official place to buy

During the presale, $OZ is available only at ozak.ai. Bookmark it. Every other site claiming to sell $OZ is a scam.

Before you start

A personal walletOne you control, such as MetaMask or Rabby. Not an exchange account.
ETH for gas$5 to $10 worth, even if you are paying in USDT or USDC.
The right networkEthereum Mainnet. Check before you start, not after.

Two mistakes that lose money

✕Sending from an exchangeTransfers sent directly from Coinbase, Binance or any exchange to the presale contract will not credit you tokens. Move funds to your own wallet first, then connect it.
✕Skipping the gasBuying with USDT or USDC still costs ETH for the network fee. With zero ETH the transaction fails and you lose the attempt.

Step 1 - get your wallet ready​

Your wallet is where your $OZ will eventually live.

Never share your seed phrase

Your Secret Recovery Phrase is the master key to your funds. Write it down, store it offline, and never type it into a website or send it to anyone. Nobody from Ozak AI will ever ask for it.

If you do not have a wallet yet, MetaMask is the straightforward choice:

  1. Download it from the official site, metamask.io
  2. Follow the setup and create a new wallet
  3. Write down your Secret Recovery Phrase and store it offline
  4. Confirm the network is set to Ethereum Mainnet

Step 2 - fund your wallet​

You can pay with ETH, USDT or USDC, plus a little extra ETH for gas.

Buying with a card

The simplest route if you are new. Use a reputable on-ramp to buy crypto with a debit or credit card and send it to your wallet address, the one beginning 0x. Copy the address from the button at the top of your wallet.

Bridging from another network

Already holding crypto on BNB Chain, Polygon or similar? Use a bridge to move it to Ethereum first.

Step 3 - buy your $OZ​

1Go to ozak.aiAnd bookmark it.
2Connect your walletClick Connect Wallet, pick your wallet, and approve the connection. You may also be asked to sign a message proving you own the address - this is standard and costs nothing.
3Choose your payment methodETH, USDT or USDC.
4Enter an amountThe dashboard shows how much $OZ you will receive as you type. Use Max to spend your full balance.
5ConfirmYour wallet shows the total, purchase plus gas. Review it and confirm.
6DoneThe network confirms in roughly 15 seconds and you get a success message.

Where your tokens are now​

Your balance lives on the presale dashboard at presale.ozak.ai, under Total $OZ Bought. Connect the same wallet you bought with.

Your tokens are not in your wallet yet

That is normal. Presale balances are held on the dashboard and become claimable at the Token Generation Event. See Can't Find Your $OZ.
