$OZ Referral System

Share your link, earn 10% in $OZ on every purchase made through it. No cap on how many people you invite.

1Get your linkConnect your wallet at token.ozak.ai and a unique referral link is generated automatically.
2Share itX, Telegram, direct messages, anywhere. There is no limit on invitations.
3Your friend buysThe smart contract records the transaction against your link automatically.
4You earnA bonus worth 10% of their purchase is credited to your presale dashboard in $OZ.10% in $OZ

Example: a friend uses your link and buys $500 of $OZ. You receive an extra $50 in $OZ.

Where the bonuses come from​

The bonuses are not minted on demand. A dedicated pool of $1,000,000 worth of $OZ funds the referral programme, and every 10% bonus is paid from it.

The programme runs until that pool is used up or the presale ends, whichever comes first.

Sharing your link​

Share honestly

Describe what Ozak AI does and let people decide. Do not promise returns, predict prices, or imply a purchase is guaranteed to gain value - no one can know that, and it puts both you and the project at risk.

Different audiences live in different places, and one post is not a campaign.

ChannelWhat works there
XShort threads explaining what the product actually does
Telegram and WhatsAppHigh-trust channels, where a genuine recommendation carries weight
RedditResearch-minded readers. Depth beats hype
DiscordCrypto communities. Read the rules on self-promotion first

The referrals that convert come from content people found useful: a short screen recording of how you bought and what the dashboard looks like, or a written explainer of what Eon does.

Post native, link second

Most platforms suppress posts that send people away. Publish the content itself first, then put your link in a comment or your bio.

FAQs​

Do I need to buy tokens before sharing my link?

No. You can generate your link and start sharing immediately.

Is the bonus paid in cash or tokens?

Tokens. Bonuses are added to your presale dashboard in $OZ and become claimable alongside anything you purchased.

What if my friend forgets to use my link?

Blockchain transactions are final and recorded as they happen, so a bonus cannot be added afterwards. The purchase has to go through your link.
