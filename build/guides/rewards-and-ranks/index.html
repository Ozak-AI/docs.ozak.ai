Rewards & Ranks

Two separate systems run alongside the presale.

No purchase needed

Rewards Hub

Complete quests to earn XP. Your share of the total XP decides your slice of a $1,000,000 pool of $OZ.

Open to everyone

Based on purchases

Ranks

Your rank comes from the USD value of what you have bought in the presale, and unlocks perks, access and status.

Five tiers

You can take part in both, and doing well in one has no effect on the other.

Rewards Hub​

Earn $OZ without buying any.

Prize pool

$1,000,000

Worth of $OZ, set aside for community members who complete quests.

How it works​

How you earnComplete quests, each worth a set amount of XP
How it pays outYour share of the total XP earned by everyone
WhenCalculated and distributed at the Token Generation Event

Worked example. If the whole community earns 100,000 XP and you earned 1,000 of it, that is 1% of the total, so you receive 1% of the pool - $10,000 worth of $OZ.

Your share is relative

Because payouts are proportional, your reward depends on both your own XP and everyone else's. Earning early and consistently matters more than a single big push.

The quests​

Quests rotate. New ones get added, campaigns come and go, and partner quests appear during Alliance Series rounds, so the live list is the only accurate one.

See the current quests and their XP values at presale.ozak.ai/rewards.

Broadly they fall into four groups:

SocialFollowing, resharing and engaging with official posts.
CommunityJoining our channels.
PlatformLogging in, completing your profile, linking your wallet.
ContentPolls and surveys, plus original posts and threads you write yourself.
Where the points actually are

Content quests are worth many multiples of a single social action, and the daily quests compound. Check the hub for what is live and what it pays.

Getting started​

1Connect your walletUse an ERC-20 wallet such as MetaMask, so your XP is tracked against your address and you can claim later.
2Open the Rewards HubYour dashboard shows current XP and every available quest.
3Complete questsCome back regularly, since daily quests reset and new campaigns get added.

Ranks​

Your rank is based on the bought USD value of the tokens in your presale wallet. Direct purchases only.

FAQs​

When does the Rewards Hub end?

This phase runs until the presale concludes, just before the TGE. The end date is announced on our social channels.

Do I need to buy or hold $OZ to earn XP?

No. The Rewards Hub is open to everyone. Ranks are the part that depends on purchases.

How do I know my XP was counted?

Your balance updates on the dashboard, in real time for most activities.

Can I repeat a quest?

Some quests are one-time and some repeat daily or per post. Each quest states its own limit on the hub.
