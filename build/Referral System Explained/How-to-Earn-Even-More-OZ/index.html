How to Earn Even More $OZ

Here are proven tactics the community is using to 10× their referral rewards and climb the ranking ladder faster.

Create Original Content (Value → Trust → Clicks)​

  • Long-form blog post - Explain Ozak AI’s predictive-model stack; end with a “Buy at presale price” button tied to your link.
  • YouTube explainer - Record a 5-minute screen share: Here’s how I bought, here’s my rank, here’s my 10 % bonus. Put the link in the video description and as a pinned comment.
  • TikTok / Reels - Quick 30-second clips with big captions like “Earn 10 % FREE TOKENS for every friend”.
Pro tip:

Platforms boost native uploads over external links. Post the content first, then add your referral URL in the comments or bio.

Sync Posts With Key Presale Milestones​

  • People FOMO hardest when the price is about to jump.
  • Schedule tweets right before each phase starts (dashboard shows countdown).
  • Share screenshots of Phase-to-Phase ROI: “Bought at $0.005, next phase $0.01 - locks in 100% upside.”

Leverage Community Events​

  • Host a Spaces / Twitter Audio - Invite a core-team member or use previous AMAs. Live Q&A converts lurkers.
  • Run mini-giveaways - e.g., “Comment your wallet, I’ll airdrop 1 % of my bonus to one random buyer via my link.”
  • Local meet-ups / university clubs - Present a short deck; QR code on the last slide links to your referral.
