FAQ (Contract Edition)​

Can I call the contract directly from Etherscan?

Yes - paste the verified address into Etherscan, choose “Write Contract,” connect your

wallet, and invoke buyTokensETH() or buyTokens().

What happens if the presale hard cap isn’t reached?

Unsold tokens are automatically sent to the Ecosystem pool after the claim window closes.
