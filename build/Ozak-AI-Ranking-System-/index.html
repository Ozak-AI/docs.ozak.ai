Ozak AI Ranking System

At Ozak AI, we believe in recognizing and rewarding our most committed community members. That’s where our gamified ranking ladder comes in.

Think of it as a way to level up your status. As you accumulate more $OZ, you’ll climb from one rank to the next, unlocking exclusive perks and gaining more influence within the ecosystem.

How the Ranking System Works​

The rule is simple: your rank is based on the bought USD value of the tokens in your presale wallet.

It only includes your direct purchases.

Meet the Ranks​

Each rank represents a new level of commitment and comes with its own set of privileges. See where you stand and what you can aim for:

RankSpend RangeMindset & Perks
Omega$0 – $99The Curious Observer. A perfect start to explore the ecosystem.
Delta$100 – $999The Diligent Learner. Gets you on the public leaderboard and gives you early access to community AMAs in future.
Beta$1,000 – $9,999The Strategic Player. Unlocks priority support and gives you access to beta-test new features first.
Alpha$10,000 – $49,999The Influential Leader. Earns you a VIP role in our community channels and a larger share in future giveaways.
Sigma$50,000+The Visionary. Grants you exclusive access with the team and priority access to any future airdrops.

How to Climb the Ladder Faster​

Combine Your Purchases: Your rank is based on your cumulative total. Two separate purchases of $600 will still move you from the Delta to the Beta rank.

You can check your current standing at any time on the live leaderboard on the app.ozak.ai page.

Good luck, and we'll see you at the top!
