Home: Asking Eon Anything

Home is a prompt box, not a dashboard. Type your question the way you would say it out loud.

Two selectors sit beneath the box: a scope, for example global crypto, and a coin, if you want the question pointed at one asset.

Questions that work well​

Is Bitcoin currently in a bullish or bearish phase based on recent market structure?

Analyze BTC using RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands for short-term momentum direction.

What does it mean if ETH's 9-day EMA crosses below the 50-day EMA while volume is declining?

Identify key support and resistance levels for ADA USDT using the Donchian Channel and ATR.

What an answer looks like​

Eon does not return a wall of numbers. A typical response reads like an analyst note:

1Current status - the one-line read
2Price structure and momentum - where price sits and the levels either side
3Volume and market structure - including whether volatility is compressing
4Broader context - recent developments and news sentiment
5Assessment - where it commits to a view
6Key levels to watch - the prices that would confirm a move either way

Every answer carries its sources so you can check the work, plus copy and feedback controls. Below it, Eon suggests follow-up questions, and the conversation stays open.

It will tell you to wait

If the market is consolidating and there is no clean setup, Eon says so rather than inventing a trade. A tool that only ever finds reasons to trade is not being straight with you.

Every conversation is saved to Threads.
