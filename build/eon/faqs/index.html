Eon FAQs & Troubleshooting

Custody, accuracy, credits, wallets, and the things that go wrong.

Using Eon

Does Eon hold my funds?

No. Eon is non-custodial. Portfolio analysis is read-only, and every trade goes through your own wallet with your approval.

Is Eon accurate?

Eon publishes its own track record. Under Signals → Prediction Markets you can see how every resolved call turned out. Nothing in Eon is a guarantee, and every signal arrives with a conviction level and its reasoning so you can weigh it yourself.

Will Eon trade for me automatically?

No. Eon surfaces analysis, signals and setups. You decide what to act on and you authorise every trade.

What does a query cost?

It depends on depth. A full analysis costs more than a quick lookup, and you are warned before anything expensive runs.

Do I need Premium?

No. Free includes the AI agents, discovery, most of the research suite, the screener, one-wallet portfolio analysis and spot trading. Premium adds Signals, the advanced research pages, perps and prediction markets, and alerts.

Where do prediction market odds come from?

Polymarket.

Can I buy credits without subscribing?

Yes, instantly.

Do I need a wallet?

You can sign in with Google or email, but a Web3 wallet unlocks portfolio analysis and trading.

Is there a mobile app?

Eon is fully responsive in a mobile browser. Nothing to download.

Troubleshooting

Where did the presale dashboard go?

To presale.ozak.ai. app.ozak.ai is now Eon. Buying $OZ is still at ozak.ai.

A page will not load or data looks stale.

Refresh first. If it persists, try a supported browser (latest Chrome, Brave, Safari or Edge) and clear your cache. Still stuck? Email support@ozak.ai with the page and a screenshot.

My wallet will not connect.

Check you are on a supported chain and that your wallet extension is unlocked. Disconnect and reconnect from the wallet side, then retry.
