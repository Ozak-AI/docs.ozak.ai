FAQs and Community

Have questions? You're in the right place.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)​

1. When will buyers receive their OZ tokens?​

The allocation is visible on the presale dashboard immediately after purchase. The tokens will become claim-able to a wallet of your choice during the Token Generation Event (TGE), which occurs shortly after the presale finishes. An exact TGE date will be published well ahead of time, and a one-click claim button will be provided.

2. How does the referral bonus work?​

You can share your unique referral link from the dashboard. When a person makes a purchase using that link, you will get 10% of their purchase in $OZ. This bonus is credited to your dashboard balance instantly and is released at the TGE with your other tokens.

3. What can I use to buy OZ in the presale?​

We are currently accepting ETH, USDT, and USDC on the Ethereum (ERC-20) network. Your OZ balance will update within minutes after you send the funds.

4. Is there a lock-up or vesting for presale tokens?​

Presale tokens do not have any additional vesting beyond the 1-month cliff and 6-month linear release outlined in the tokenomics. The first 10% of your tokens unlocks at the TGE, with the rest vesting on a monthly basis over six months.

5. How has the price changed across phases?​

  • Phase 1: $0.001 (completed)
  • Phase 2: $0.002 (completed)
  • Phase 3: $0.003 (completed)
  • Phase 4: $0.005 (completed)
  • Phase 5: $0.01 (completed)
  • Phase 6: $0.012 (completed)
  • Phase 7: $0.014 (live)

Each phase concludes automatically once its supply cap is met.

6. When will presale participants get exclusive access to the Ozak AI platform?​

Presale purchasers will be the first group to access the platform. Access will be provided in stages, starting with the main dashboard, then the Prediction Agent features, and finally the complete suite of trading tools. All updates and links for entry will be shared through our social channels.

7. When and where will $OZ be listed?​

The initial listings are scheduled to go live right after the Token Generation Event (TGE). We are in the final stages of agreements with top-tier exchanges. The specific trading pairs and launch times will be announced 7-10 days prior to the TGE on our official channels.

8. What is the official token contract address?​

To maintain a clean audit trail and prevent fake tokens, the final $OZ ERC-20 contract is only deployed at the TGE. Until that time, all presale deposits go through the verified Presale Contract address. Please bookmark our official channels, as we will publish the official token contract address as soon as it is live.

9. Have other questions?​

You can contact us at any time at support@ozak.ai
