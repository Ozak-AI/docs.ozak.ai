Cliff and vesting, in plain terms​

Cliff is a waiting period after launch before any tokens from a bucket start releasing. The team has a 3-month cliff, so no team tokens move for the first three months.

Vesting is the gradual release after the cliff. Rather than arriving at once, tokens release steadily across the period. Team tokens release in equal monthly amounts over 24 months.

What the schedule is designed to prevent​

No large team unlocksTeam and Reserve carry the longest vesting. The team waits months to access anything, then receives it slowly across two years.
A market from day oneA full 10% of supply is dedicated to exchange liquidity at launch.
Enforced in codeThe release schedule is written into audited smart contracts, so it executes automatically rather than on trust.

Risks, and what we do about them​

Every project carries risk. These are the ones we plan around.

RiskMitigation
Large sell pressure at unlocksUnlocks are gradual and linear month by month rather than in chunks, and launch liquidity helps the market absorb new supply
AI models underperforming over timeThe 30% ecosystem allocation exists to fund data scientists and developers to keep building, testing and improving the models
Smart contract bugs or exploitsContracts undergo independent third-party audits before launch. See Contract Audits

The intent is a loop rather than a one-way extraction: holders provide capital and participation, builders receive grants to create tools, those tools generate fees, and the token captures that value.
